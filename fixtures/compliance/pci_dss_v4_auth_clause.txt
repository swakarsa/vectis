REQUIREMENT 10.2.1 — AUDIT LOG IDENTITY CONTINUITY & TRANSACTION LINKAGE:
All authentication and session objects utilized in transaction authorization
and downstream settlement clearing MUST maintain immutable, continuous linkage
to the original user identity. Renaming identifier attributes from 'id' to 'sub'
without backward-compatible bridge shims compromises audit trail continuity,
constituting a Critical Finding under PCI-DSS Requirement 10.2.1.

REQUIREMENT 3.4.2 — DOWNSTREAM SYSTEM SETTLEMENT INTEGRITY:
Any refactor or token modernization migrating away from plaintext session account
identifiers MUST maintain backward-compatible contractual integrity with downstream
financial settlement workers and clearing nodes. A refactor that silently breaks
settlement worker ledger routing is classified as a "Critical Non-Compliance Event" (SEV-1).

REQUIREMENT 8.2.8 — SESSION DEPRECATION GRACE PERIOD:
When migrating from legacy user session objects (LegacyUserSession) to modern
token-based representations (SessionUser), the architecture MUST provide a minimum
90-day deprecation bridge or compatibility shim. Direct abrupt deletion of contract
properties (.id, .accountNumber, .tier) that forces downstream service failure is forbidden.
